Decision: product analytics uses Amplitude. Google Analytics 4 was rejected as the main product analytics tool for the launch.

Reasons: generous free tier covers 10M events a month; behavioural cohorts and user-level paths, unsampled, for following shoppers through the store; warehouse export included, so no separate pipeline is needed for later analysis.

Decided by Tomas, approved by Nia.
