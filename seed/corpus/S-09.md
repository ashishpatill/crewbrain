Assumptions: 120 GB of original video at launch, growing about 40 GB a month. Students stream through the CDN, about 2 TB of egress a month by December.

Backblaze B2: about $6 per TB per month stored. Egress to Cloudflare is free under the Bandwidth Alliance. Estimated December bill: under $2 a month for storage, $0 egress.

AWS S3 Standard: about $23 per TB per month stored, plus about $90 per TB of internet egress. Estimated December bill: around $180 a month, almost all of it egress.

Neon large objects: storage above the free 0.5 GB means a paid plan, and video bytes would also count against compute and backups. Not a realistic home for 120 GB.
