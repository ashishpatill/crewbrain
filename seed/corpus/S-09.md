Assumptions: 120 GB of original product photos and lookbook video at launch, growing about 40 GB a month. Shoppers load media through the CDN, about 2 TB of egress a month by December.

Amazon S3: about $23 per TB per month stored, with lifecycle tiering to cheaper classes as originals age. With CloudFront in front for delivery, the estimated December bill for this option stays predictable as lookbook traffic grows.

Google Cloud Storage: about $20 per TB per month stored, plus about $120 per TB of internet egress when shoppers download directly. At lookbook-launch spikes that direct egress adds up quickly, so routing delivery through a CDN would cost a fraction of direct egress. Estimated December bill: well over $200 a month, almost all of it egress.

MySQL large objects: storing 120 GB of media bytes in the orders database would bloat it and slow backups. Keeping media bytes out of MySQL keeps the orders database small and backups fast, so it is not a realistic home for 120 GB.
