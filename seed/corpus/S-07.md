Decision: Mux handles encoding, adaptive HLS playback and viewer analytics for Video Lessons. Self-hosted ffmpeg workers were rejected.

Reasons: saves several weeks of building queues and a player; adaptive bitrate out of the box; built-in viewer analytics.

Mux keeps its own encoded renditions. We still need our own durable store for the original uploads.
