Decision: Cloudinary handles product photo and lookbook media for Harborlight Outfitters, with a built-in media library for merchandisers. Imgix was considered and rejected.

Reasons: saves several weeks versus building an upload pipeline; on-the-fly responsive transforms for photos and video at every screen size.

Cloudinary keeps its own processed renditions. We still need our own durable store for the original uploads.
