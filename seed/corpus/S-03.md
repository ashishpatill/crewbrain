Decision: MySQL is the primary relational database for Harborlight Outfitters. MongoDB was considered and rejected.

Reasons: our data is relational, covering orders, stock and payments; schema migrations live in reviewable SQL files, so changes can be checked before they run.

Constraint to remember: keep large media bytes out of MySQL, so the orders database stays small and backups stay fast.
