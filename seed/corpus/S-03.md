Decision: Neon Postgres is the primary relational database for Acme Learning. PlanetScale was considered and rejected.

Reasons: relational data model; free plan to start; database branching per pull request for safe migrations.

Constraint to remember: the Neon free plan caps storage at 0.5 GB per project. Keep large blobs out of the database.
