Decision: the Mobile App uses Flutter. React Native was rejected.

Reasons: the app shares the Dart size-guide and pricing packages with the internal tools app; one codebase covers both stores, with no over-the-air updates but staged rollouts instead; and the AR prototype held 60fps in the chosen stack and dropped frames in the alternative.

Decided by Lena, approved by Nia.
