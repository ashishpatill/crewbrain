Omar: Read Priya's cost sheet. Google Cloud Storage came out slightly cheaper per GB stored, but Amazon S3 storage costs about $23 per TB a month, with lifecycle tiering to cheaper classes as originals age.

Omar: Putting photos and lookbook video in MySQL would fill the database on the first day of uploads. Keeping media bytes out of MySQL keeps the orders database small and backups fast: product rows, not media bytes.

Omar: With CloudFront in front, egress through CloudFront is a fraction of direct S3 egress at lookbook-launch spikes. Proposal: original uploads go to an Amazon S3 bucket called media-originals-prod, and MySQL keeps only metadata and object keys.
