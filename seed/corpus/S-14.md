Decision: Checkout payments use Stripe. Adyen was rejected for now because its enterprise onboarding did not fit the launch timeline.

Reasons: PCI SAQ-A, so card data never touches our servers; Apple Pay and wallets at checkout; live in days, not weeks, in the test setup. Fulfilment labels run through Shippo once the order is paid.

Decided by Priya, approved by Nia.
