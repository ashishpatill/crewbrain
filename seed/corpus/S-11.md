Decision: original lesson videos are stored in the Backblaze B2 bucket lessons-prod. Neon Postgres stores only lesson metadata and the B2 object key. Rejected: Neon large objects, AWS S3.

Reason 1 (cost): B2 storage is about $6 per TB per month against about $23 for S3, see the August cost sheet.

Reason 2 (egress): B2 egress through Cloudflare is free under the Bandwidth Alliance; S3 egress at our expected 2 TB a month would cost around $180.

Reason 3 (database limit): video bytes in Neon would exceed the 0.5 GB free plan immediately and bloat backups and branches.

Decided by Ravi, approved by Asha on 12 Aug 2026.
