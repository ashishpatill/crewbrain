Decision: lookbook photo and video originals are stored in Amazon S3 in the media-originals-prod bucket. MySQL stores only product metadata and the S3 object key. Rejected: Google Cloud Storage and keeping media bytes inside MySQL.

Reason 1 (cost): Amazon S3 storage costs about $23 per TB a month, with lifecycle tiering to cheaper classes for older lookbook originals after the autumn launch. Google Cloud Storage was priced in the same range on the August cost sheet, so the deciding factor was not headline storage price alone.

Reason 2 (egress): At lookbook-launch spikes, egress through Amazon CloudFront is a fraction of direct Amazon S3 egress, because cached product photos and videos are served from the edge instead of repeatedly leaving S3. Google Cloud Storage would have needed a separate delivery setup to match that pattern.

Reason 3 (risk): Keeping media bytes out of MySQL keeps the orders database small and backups fast, so checkout and stock tables stay quick to snapshot and restore during the launch.

Decided by Omar, approved by Nia on 12 Aug 2026.
